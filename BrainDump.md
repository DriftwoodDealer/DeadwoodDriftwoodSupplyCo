## Inbox
Add new thoughts here first.

* 2026-05-16 - Storefront direction: keep the site dark, gorgeous, artisan, river-rooted, and premium. It should feel like the Apple/Gucci version of driftwood, not Shopify, eBay, or a cheap template. Top of the storefront should have slots for three featured pieces. Reptile/bioactive sector should include size classes: Nano, Medium, Specimen XL, Centerpiece XXL, and Tree XXXL, with useful descriptions for approximate size and best-fit use cases. Need storytelling around sourcing, provenance, and the river. Need a visible treatment/safety section saying every piece is power washed and heat treated at 275 degrees for 4+ hours. Keep future "try this piece in your tank" tool in mind while designing product data and UI. Need normal/generic product areas plus a premium rare-piece area, maybe called Vault, Masterclass, or a better name, for standout pieces with extra photoshoots, special attention, GPS coordinates when available, and video support. Vault product popup idea: image gallery on the left, thumbnails underneath, text/details on the right, add to cart/buy now or inquiry action. Need wholesale area where shops can apply, provide business info, get verified, then access private wholesale pricing. Wholesale should support subscriptions/contracts for recurring shipments every set number of weeks/months/year, with bigger discounts for longer contracts. Goal is to prevent unverified bulk resellers from buying large quantities. Need custom request form for landscaping/taxidermy: user can specify length, width, shape/style, and desired use; requests become a scavenging lookout list. CMS/backend should eventually allow video on any product, similar to Depop/Facebook Marketplace media handling.
* 2026-05-16 - Wholesale verification needs to be stringent before anyone gets private access. Need business validation, reseller prevention, and permissioned wholesale pages/pricing.
* 2026-05-17 - Custom request submissions should eventually feed a private field-lookout app/list visible only to Mark and John. The request should capture buyer need, dimensions, shape/style, use case, and notes, then turn that into a scavenging target list for river walks.
* 2026-05-17 - Treatment protocol should have a premium learn-more path later with more process steps and video showing power washing, heating, and prep work. Sculptural/art pieces should emphasize engraved provenance such as latitude/longitude and river notes. Large landscaping pieces may support custom plaques with house number, last name, property marker, or origin detail. Brand lore should lean into the Mississippi/Missouri river mythology in an elegant global way, not cheesy patriotic copy.
* 2026-05-17 - Possible homepage polish later: tiny icons near the four "ways into the river archive" cards. Mark may provide icons. Do not add generic icons without approval.
